|---|---|

## 1. 一张新图：固定段落 + 一句动作

拼法（`tools/g1_r9_prompts.py`）：

```text
角色段（RIN / LEN_YOUNG）+ 手（RIN_HANDS / LEN_HANDS）+ SHEET_RULE + 画幅（*_FRAME）
+ 一句动作 + [TABLE / PAPER / TEAR] + STYLE + NEVER + KEY
参考图：人设图（垫成 2:3）→ 道具设定图 → 一张只管取景的图
```

### 角色

```text
THE ANDROID GIRL (Rin; her character sheet is attached): 14 years old, 152 cm, slim, not chibi. Short golden-blond bob in a few
big locks; one big stiff white ribbon bow on top of her head (two loops, one small knot); two parallel white hair clips on HER
OWN LEFT side of the fringe; a round white ear unit over each ear; teal eyes (#218EA3) with flat concentric rings; fair cream
skin. White sleeveless dress with a dark-navy sailor collar edged with one yellow line, a yellow neckerchief tied in front, a
small dark round socket in the middle of her chest just under the collar (an empty ring, as on the sheet). Her arms and legs
are smooth white doll limbs with plain white SPHERE joints at shoulder, elbow, wrist and knee and NOTHING else on them: no
screws, no screw holes, no panel lines. The "02" in dark navy on her LEFT upper arm. Dark-navy flared boots with a yellow band
at the top.
```

得到“心”之前，她的脸另加一句：

```text
She has no heart yet: her face is completely EXPRESSIONLESS, the mouth one short straight flat line, eyebrows level, no smile, no blush.
```

### 画法

```text
DRAWING: exactly the drawing of the attached character sheets: an even, thin, crisp dark Prussian-blue outline; flat cel
colours; ONE hard-edged cool grey-blue shadow shape per form; hair as a few big smooth locks lying close to the head, not a
burst of thin spiky strands; few cloth folds; eyes drawn like the eyes on the sheets. No gradients, no shine, no sparkles,
no blush, no noise, no texture, no hatching, no blur.
```

### 手（照《少女A》的 `HANDS` 改写，铃和科学家各一段）

```text
Her hands, wherever one shows: the small hands of her sheet: smooth white doll hands joined to the forearm by a white sphere
wrist joint; a narrow palm, four slim fingers and one thumb with simple rounded tips; nothing drawn inside the hand (no
creases, no knuckle lines, no nails); at most one simple hard-edged shadow shape on a hand. Each hand is the correct left
or right hand for its own arm, with the thumb on the correct side.
```

```text
His hands, wherever one shows: a slim man's hands drawn as simply as his face: narrow palm, four slim fingers and one thumb,
a clean outline with at most a couple of short inner lines, no veins, no tendons, no knuckle detail, the nails at most a tiny
simple shape; a hand is about as long as his face from eyebrows to chin, never bigger. A nearer hand gets a little bigger,
never more detailed. Each hand is the correct left or right hand for its own arm, joined at the wrist to its own forearm and
rolled white sleeve, with the thumb on the correct side.
```

### 设定图说了算、禁止项、底色

```text
The attached character sheets decide how the two look: stay true to them and do not improve on them. The picture attached
LAST only shows the framing and how big the figures are in the picture; its drawing has mistakes (hands, cut-out edges), so
do not copy its hands, its arms or its details.
```

```text
Must NOT have: extra or missing fingers; two left hands or two right hands on one person; a hand not joined to its own arm; any
cut-out or pasted look; hair broken into many thin strands; gradients, glow or soft shadows; screws or screw holes on her;
floating objects.
```

```text
BACKGROUND: one perfectly flat solid magenta #FF00FF from edge to edge, because the figures will be cut out: no floor, no
ground line, no cast shadow, no wall, no frame, no motion lines, no watermark, no text other than the "02" on her upper arm
(and the paper's marks where the text asks for them).
```

### 道具：桌子、纸

```text
THE TABLE is exactly the plain lab table of the attached prop sheet: a flat, LEVEL, rectangular top in pale grey-blue (#CBD4E6),
4 cm thick, with a thin dark outline and one darker grey-blue (#7C8AAC) band on its front edge, straight square legs; no drawers,
nothing slanted.
```

纸写清楚**哪一面朝谁、标题在哪一端、手怎么拿**（递报告那张）：

```text
THE SHEET: one stiff sheet of warm off-white A4 paper (not cyan), held almost level in front of her chest, tilted a little toward
us so that we see its printed upper face, squeezed by the perspective into a long narrow shape. The TOP of the page is at the FAR
end of the sheet, away from her, toward the person she offers it to (on the viewer's left) … The BOTTOM of the page, at HER end,
near her hands, is one empty rectangular box. Nothing else is printed: no other letters, no scribbles, no fake text.
HOW SHE HOLDS IT: with BOTH hands, the respectful Japanese way, at her end of the sheet: each hand supports one corner of her
end from below, the fingers under the sheet and the thumb on top of it …
```

### 一句动作

动作只写人会怎么说的那一句，不写像素和角度。例如开场他给她系蝴蝶结、教她笑：

```text
He is tying the big white bow on top of her head: both his hands are at the bow, his nearer hand pinching the outer loop on the
viewer's left and his other hand pinching the knot in the middle, pulling it tight; fingers on the front of the cloth, thumbs
behind it. The bow keeps its size and shape. His arms do not cover her face. He looks up at the bow with a small absorbed smile.
```

```text
She copies him like a machine: she raises both her doll hands to her face … and with her two INDEX fingers presses the corners
of her own mouth upward … Her eyes stay wide open, round, teal, EMPTY, staring straight ahead; eyebrows unmoved. Her closed mouth
stays small and flat in the middle (she is not smiling) and only its two ends are dragged up where each fingertip presses.
```

## 2. 同一张母版，原样重画、只改一处

同组的后续每一张都以母版为第一张参考图（`tools/v2_edits.ps1` 的 `$sameR`）：

```text
Redraw the FIRST attached image EXACTLY: the same android girl, the same view, the same picture size and framing, the same SIZE
and POSITION of her head and body in the picture, the same proportions (do not change the length or thickness of any limb), the
same face and the same EYES (shape, size, teal color, concentric ring), hair, hair clips, bow, ear units, collar, neckerchief,
dress, sphere joints and dark-blue flared boots, the same line weight, colors, flat fills and shadow shapes, the same flat magenta
(#FF00FF) background, no ground, no cast shadow, no motion lines, no blur, no text other than the "02" on the android's upper arm,
no watermark. The whole figure stays inside the picture. Anything not named below must be traced unchanged from the first image.
CHANGE ONLY THIS:
```

后面接一句，例如走路的关键张：

```text
seen from behind, she is WALKING away from the viewer with a slow even step. PASSING: she stands on her LEFT leg, straight; her
RIGHT leg is lifted with the knee bent, its boot passing beside the left calf, clear of the ground. Both arms hang at her sides.
Head and body upright.
```

手臂类的改动另加一句：“The arms are exactly as long as in the first image: only their angle changes.”

## 3. 中间张：看前后两张

```text
This is ONE IN-BETWEEN drawing for hand-drawn animation. The FIRST attached image is the finished drawing just BEFORE this one,
the SECOND attached image is the finished drawing just AFTER it, in the same shot from the same fixed camera. Match them exactly:
the same picture size and framing, the same SIZE and POSITION of the head(s) and body(ies) in the picture with no change of scale,
the same faces and eyes, the same line weight, colors and shadow shapes, … Whatever is the same in both drawings must be traced
unchanged; everything that moves is part way between the two drawings; nothing new appears. THIS MOMENT:
half way between the two: her left leg carries her weight; her right leg has just left the ground behind, knee beginning to bend;
the arms half way back to her sides.
```

## 4. 风的循环

母版逐线描 + 只许风动的清单 + 这一相位（`tools/w1_wind_prompts.py`）：

```text
ONLY THE WIND CHANGES, and the wind moves only these: the ends of her hair (the locks below the ear units and the loose tips
around her face; the bangs only at their very tips), the two loops and the hanging tail ends of the big white bow (the knot stays
in place, the loops keep their size), the two tails of the yellow neckerchief (the knot stays), and the hem of the white dress
below the hips. The wind blows sideways across the picture, from the viewer's left toward the viewer's right.
CHANGE ONLY THIS: PHASE 2, the gust at its strongest: everything that the wind moves is blown clearly toward the viewer's RIGHT,
clearly further than in the first image but not wild - the hair on the viewer's right streams out to the right in a few big locks,
their points reaching out about half a head-width further than in the first image …
```

中间张参考三张图（母版、前一张、后一张）：

```text
THIS MOMENT: exactly half way between the second and the third attached images. Every hair lock, bow loop, neckerchief tail and
the dress hem is drawn in the position half way between where it is in those two drawings - the same shapes, part way along
their swing; nothing new appears. Her body, arms, hands, face and boots are the first attached image, unchanged.
```

## 5. 结尾的泪：每一帧以上一帧为底

开头说明“这是一滴泪的慢动画的一帧，上一帧里已经在脸上的水都照描”，然后只写这一帧的变化，最后统一泪的画法（`tools/w2_endt.py` 的 `PRE`、`CH`、`STYLE`）：

```text
CHANGE ONLY ONE THING: the tear from the viewer's LEFT eye has run further down the SAME path: the streak is longer and its
teardrop-shaped tip is now LEVEL WITH HER MOUTH, still on the cheek a little inward from the face outline. The upper part of the
streak that was already there stays exactly where it is, the same width.
```

```text
Tear style, the same as the rest of this flat drawing: the water is a flat very pale blue-white fill edged by a thin dark
Prussian-blue line (thinner than the face outline). A running tear is ONE continuous soft shape: a narrow wet streak on the skin
that is thinnest at the eye corner and widens a little as it goes down, flowing WITHOUT ANY NECK OR STEP straight into its lower
end, which is a teardrop-shaped tip … never a ball on a stick, never a pin, lollipop or microphone shape … The water always lies
ON her skin, inside the outline of her face. … The two sides of her face are not mirror copies.
```

## 写提示词时的几条经验

- **设定图比越来越长的提示词更管用。** 角色段落写清身份和不可改的特征就够了，长相交给设定图。
- **手单独立规矩**：小、细、平涂、手里不画线；近处的手变大但不变复杂；每只手是它自己那条胳膊的左手或右手。
- **说“不要什么”要具体**：“never a ball on a stick, never a pin, lollipop or microphone shape”比“画得自然”有用得多。
- **画幅写死**：“exactly the same pixel size and shape as the first attached image (W × H pixels)”。参考图先垫成模型常用的画幅（`tools/pad_ref.py`），否则它会换一个画幅，比例跟着变。
- **一次只改一处。** 一张图里要改三件事，就写“CHANGE THREE THINGS. First … Second … Third …”，并逐条说清其余照描。
